Ten seconds

at Chef Fatso

Chef Fatso is an open-air cafe styled like a Bali and Goa holiday. Thatched cottages, lush palms and a small pool at the centre, with acoustic music drifting in as the lights come on. Pure vegetarian Continental, Italian, Mexican and fusion plates, made for sharing late into the night.
